Logline
When a super-villain threatens to destroy the Sydney Opera House, the Prime Minister calls in Spider Mate — who uses Quote Mate to save the day before the bomb goes off.

Synopsis (Full Story)
Part 1 — The Crisis

In the heart of Canberra, the Prime Minister's office is in chaos. A super-villain has planted a bomb inside the Sydney Opera House and is threatening to detonate it unless their demands are met. The clock is ticking. The National Security Adviser is on the phone, panicking.

PM: "GET SPIDER MATE IN HERE! NOW!"

Part 2 — The Call

Spider Mate answers the call. He's calm, professional, and unfazed. He listens to the briefing and lays out the plan:

"Evacuate everyone from inside and surrounding the Opera House. Bring the target back to the Defense Minister alive. And stop the bomb from going off."

The Defense Minister asks how fast he can do it.

"Quote will be in your inbox in 10 seconds."

Part 3 — The Quote

Spider Mate pulls out his phone and opens Quote Mate. He speaks the job details naturally, almost casually:

"Evacuate Opera House: 1 million dollars. Bring target back alive: 2 million dollars. Stop the bomb: 500 thousand. Call-out fee: 50 thousand. Emergency surcharge: 30%."

Quote Mate instantly populates the line items, calculates the GST, and generates a professional PDF quote — all in seconds.

Part 4 — The Quote Delivered

Spider Mate taps "Send" . The quote is delivered to the Prime Minister's office.

"Quote sent. Gotta love Quote Mate."

Part 5 — The Price Check

The Defense Minister storms into the PM's office, holding a tablet.

Defense Minister: "Prime Minister! The quote just came in. It's going to be 3.5 million dollars!"

The PM sighs, then shrugs.

"Fine. As long as he gets the job done."

Part 6 — The Rescue

Spider Mate swings into action. The bomb is stopped. The Opera House is saved. The city is safe.

Final Scene — The Call to Action

Spider Mate walks off into the sunset (or maybe just into a convenience store). Quote Mate logo appears on screen.

"Quote Mate. Fast enough for a national crisis. Try it free today."
